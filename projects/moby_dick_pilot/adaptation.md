# Moby-Dick pilot — adaptation sheet

## Intent

A 45–60 second, narration-led proof of concept built from the supplied opening
of Chapter 1, “Loomings.” The pilot keeps Melville's first-person voice and
compresses the chapter into one visual movement:

```text
melancholy on land → humanity drawn to water → choosing life before the mast
→ the white whale as an unknowable promise
```

The source text is from Herman Melville's *Moby-Dick; or, The Whale* (1851).
The user supplied the passage used for this adaptation.

## Character continuity

Ishmael is defined for this pilot as a lean white American man in his early
thirties, with a weathered angular face, dark wavy collar-length hair, a short
untrimmed beard, deep-set gray-blue eyes, a charcoal wool greatcoat over a cream
linen shirt, a faded navy neckerchief, worn brown trousers and boots.

This appearance is a production choice because the supplied passage does not
give a complete physical description. All four generated frames use the first
image as their identity/style reference.

## Shot plan

1. **Drizzly Manhattan** — Ishmael turns toward the harbor; cold, inward mood.
2. **Water-gazers** — the private impulse becomes a universal human attraction.
3. **Simple sailor** — decision becomes physical action and departure.
4. **Hooded phantom** — realistic night sea opens into the premonition of the
   white whale.

Every scene is narration/voice-over. No visible character needs precise lip
sync, which makes this a safer first LTX render while still exercising Edge TTS
audio conditioning, motion, continuity and final assembly.

## Generated image prompt set

All images use the built-in image generation path and the `historical-scene`
prompt class. Shared constraints: 16:9 cinematic first frame, photorealistic
1840s production design, exact Ishmael continuity, natural anatomy, no modern
objects, no text, no logo and no watermark.

- `01_drizzly_manhattan.png`: wet lower-Manhattan street descending to harbor.
- `02_water_gazers.png`: landsmen fixed at the edge of the Battery pier.
- `03_simple_sailor.png`: Ishmael working before the mast as the city recedes.
- `04_hooded_phantom.png`: distant white sperm whale like a snow hill in fog.
